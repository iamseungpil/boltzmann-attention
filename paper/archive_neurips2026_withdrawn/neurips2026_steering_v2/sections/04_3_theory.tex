\section{Setup and Mechanism}\label{sec:theory}

\paragraph{Notation and operators.}
Consider single-head self-attention (multi-head by direct sum). At layer $\ell$, the query is $q\in\mathbb{R}^d$, keys are $K\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times d}$, values are $V\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times d_v}$, and the perturbed keys are $\hat K = K + E$. The original output is $o(q) = \sum_t s_t(q) v_t$ with $s_t(q) = \mathrm{softmax}(qK^\top/\sqrt d)_t$. A per-head ontology basis $B_{\mathrm{ont}}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times r}$ is obtained by Gram-Schmidt-orthogonalizing the 4-facet ontology derived directly from the tool catalog, and the projector is $P_{\mathrm{ont}} = B_{\mathrm{ont}} B_{\mathrm{ont}}^\top$. Three operator families are defined:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ops}
\underbrace{K' = K + \alpha K P_{\mathrm{ont}}}_{\text{stationary K (SEKA)}},\quad
\underbrace{Q' = Q + \beta Q P_{\mathrm{ont}},\ \beta<0}_{\text{Q-coverage (ours)}},\quad
\underbrace{\ell<\lfloor\tau L\rfloor\!:K';\ \text{all }\ell\!:Q'}_{\text{layer-adaptive K+Q (ours)}}
\end{equation}
All three share the same $B_{\mathrm{ont}}$, so any difference in outcome is attributable to operator form alone. The only analytical assumption used in the paper is bounded norms: constants $Q_{\max}, V_{\max}, \rho$ exist such that $\|q\|\le Q_{\max}$, $\|v_t\|\le V_{\max}$, $\|e_t\|\le\rho$. No assumption is made about the joint distribution of $(q, K, V)$.

\paragraph{Three core theorems.}
The paper's theory reduces to three statements. Full proofs are in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}; corollaries and a conditional proposition live in Appendix~\ref{app:corollaries}.

\begin{theorem}[Stationary K is independent of emitted state]\label{thm:no-memory}
For the stationary K-side operator in~\eqref{eq:ops}, if two decoding histories $h, h'$ induce the same hidden state $x$ but differ in their already-emitted tool sets, then $K'(h) = K'(h')$.
\end{theorem}

\emph{Proof.} $K' = K + \alpha K P_{\mathrm{ont}}$ is a function only of $(K, \alpha, P_{\mathrm{ont}})$, all three of which are history-invariant. $\square$

The same argument applies to AdaSEKA (expert selection is a function of $x$, so the same $x$ yields the same transformation). The observable consequence is a rise in \texttt{repeated\_first\_tool\_rate} (Corollary~\ref{cor:seka-repeat}, Appendix).

\begin{theorem}[Geometry of the Q-projection]\label{thm:q-sign}
For $\beta < 0$ in~\eqref{eq:ops}, $q'$ scales the ontology component of $q$ uniformly by $(1+\beta)$ and preserves the orthogonal component.
\end{theorem}

\emph{Proof.} From $P_{\mathrm{ont}}^2 = P_{\mathrm{ont}}$, $q' P_{\mathrm{ont}} = (1+\beta) q P_{\mathrm{ont}}$ and $q'(I-P_{\mathrm{ont}}) = q(I-P_{\mathrm{ont}})$. $\square$

This is the simplest history-free rotation of ``trust the dominant ontology component less,'' unifying the three operators in~\eqref{eq:ops} as \emph{rotational steering on the ontology subspace}.

\begin{theorem}[Attention output: exact integral remainder]\label{thm:bound}
For $\phi(\tau) := \sum_t \mathrm{softmax}(\ell(q) + \tau\alpha(q))_t v_t$, the identity $\hat o(q) - o(q) = L(q, E) + R(q, E)$ holds exactly, and under bounded norms $\|\hat o - o\|^2 \le 2\,\mathrm{Var}_s\alpha(q)\cdot \mathrm{Var}_s[V](q) + C_1\rho^4$, with $C_1 = 2 Q_{\max}^4 V_{\max}^2/d^2$.
\end{theorem}

This is a diagnostic tool. Full forms of $L$ and $R$ and the four-step proof are in Appendix~\ref{app:proof:bound}.

\paragraph{Regime-split prediction.}
The choice $\tau = 1/4$ for the layer-adaptive operator is conditional on an empirical premise E1 (transformer depth stages: early for information encoding, middle for stabilization, late for output convergence). Combining E1 with Theorem~\ref{thm:no-memory} yields the prediction that the marginal benefit of K-imprinting decays as $(1 - \varphi(j-1))$ with tool-sequence position $j$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:ladapt-intuition} and Corollary~\ref{cor:regime}, Appendix). The prediction is tested directly by the $\tau^2$-retail smoke in \S\ref{sec:exp:performance} and by the layer-boundary sweep in Appendix~\ref{app:layer-sweep}.
