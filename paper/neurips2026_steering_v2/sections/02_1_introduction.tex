\section{1. Introduction}
Enterprise AI agents select among 10³–10⁴ tools per query. Three prevailing approaches — \textbf{fine-tuning}, \textbf{retrieval-augmented prompting}, and \textbf{activation steering} — each degrade under continual tool-addition and workflow-change, a deployment reality witnessed during the Netsru Gemma-3-27B agent engagement (Appendix E).


Activation-steering methods (CAA, ITI, PASTA, ASA, Focus Directions, AdaSEKA) introduce a rank-1 or rank-M \emph{Q-side} perturbation. We take the dual view: perturb the \emph{K} side along an ontology-derived rank-\texttt{R} basis with \textbf{per-facet independent gates}. The central empirical finding of this work is that this construction identifies a \textbf{uniquely privileged subspace}: at a matched perturbation magnitude $\alpha=0.3$, only directions within $\mathrm{span}(B_{\mathrm{ont}})$ preserve the model's structured function-calling (FC) output. Random or feature-shuffled directions of the same norm \emph{completely destroy} FC emission (F1 collapses from 0.731 to 0.000 on all 497 multi-tool queries) while the ontology direction preserves it (F1 = 0.685). The observed direction-specificity gap of +68.5pp is an order of magnitude larger than any accuracy lift we had originally predicted and is the strongest single signal in the paper.


We frame this result as a \textbf{stability property} of the rank-$R$ ontology subspace: it is the unique $R$-dimensional set of K-perturbations whose KL-divergence from the base model remains $O(\alpha^2)$, while complementary-direction perturbations of equal magnitude exit the FC-emission manifold once $\alpha > \alpha^*$. Accuracy lifts — when they occur (Subtask1 cross-model +0.1 to +6.3pp, contrastive Subtask4 +5.8pp smoke, MMLU flat $\alpha=0.2$ +1.4pp) — are supporting evidence that the direction is \emph{downstream-usable}, not the main contribution.


\subsection{1.1 Contributions (unified frame: stability + accuracy + compression Pareto)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item **Joint Pareto optimality of $B_{\mathrm{ont}}$ (unifying contribution, §3.6, Thm 6.19)**. The per-head ontology basis is \emph{simultaneously} Pareto-optimal for inference-time steering (Thm 6.17 Q-coverage accuracy with optional small-α K augmentation) and KV-cache compression (Thm 6.18 attention-weighted bit allocation) — both factoring through the same $\pi(t,f)\sigma_f^2$ matrix from a single calibration forward pass. Single-basis sufficiency (Cor 6.19.1) and zero asymptotic overhead (Cor 6.19.2). This is the unification result that bridges the steering and compression literatures. (The K-channel parameterizes only the orthogonal \emph{stability} axis of item 1 below, not the accuracy axis.)
\item \textbf{Ontology-privileged subspace stability (main verified empirical finding, §5.5, Cor 6.9.6)}. On MetaTool Subtask4 (N=497, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), real $B_{\mathrm{ont}}$ at $\alpha=0.3$ maintains F1 = 0.685 while random and feature-shuffled $B_{\mathrm{ont}}$ at the same magnitude both collapse to F1 = 0.000 — direction-specificity gap \textbf{+68.5pp}. Cross-model direction-specificity is confirmed on Subtask1 full 995 (Qwen sum gap +48.84 / mean +28.04; Llama-Base sum +7.33 / mean +3.22; codex first\_line +24.42).
\item \textbf{Theorem 6.1 (per-sample attention-weighted bound, §3.1)}. $\mathbb E_q\|\hat o - o\|^2 \le 2\mathbb E[\mathrm{qaMSE}\cdot \mathrm{Var}_s V] + C_1\rho^4$. Verified on Qwen2.5-7B L=13, $\alpha=0.3$, 2800 head-query samples: \textbf{bound\_pass\_rate 1.00}, median LHS/RHS ratio $2.36\times 10^{-8}$.
\item \textbf{Corollary 6.9 + 6.9.6 (rank separation + stability characterization, §3.3)}. Operator $\varepsilon$-numerical rank of AdaSEKA saturates at $r$; ours achieves $R = \sum_f r_f$. SVD verification (500 queries): ours 24.0 vs AdaSEKA 7.44, gap +17. \emph{Geometric strengthening (Cor 6.9.6, new)}: perturbations within the rank-$R$ ontology subspace incur KL divergence $O(\alpha^2)$; equal-magnitude perturbations in the complementary subspace cross the FC-emission manifold boundary for $\alpha \ge \alpha^*$. This corollary is the theoretical footing of contribution 1.
\item \textbf{Corollary 6.7 (soft-gate phase-closure with Hypothesis (R), §3.2)}. A Lipschitz soft-gate facet operator achieves $\mathrm{qaMSE} = O(\varepsilon_q)$. Hard gates violate (R); MMLU N=1000 ($\alpha=1.0$): flat 0.584, soft 0.614, hard\_argmax 0.552, hard\_thresh 0.535 — Lipschitz-violation degradation as predicted by Remark 6.14.A.3.
\item \textbf{Corollary 6.11 / 6.12 + Rmk 6.12.1 (hard-selection failure modes, §3.4)}. Per-token hard-selection K-quantization incurs $((R-k)/R)^2$ qaMSE penalty; composed with dense K-bias is strictly worse than either alone. Predicted and observed.
\item \textbf{Theorem 6.13 (categorical-channel compression bridge, §3.5)}. The facet basis doubles as a quantization axis. OCQ (1-bit facet + KIVI-style residual) at 1.81 avg bits beats KIVI at 2.00 bits by $-4.37$ PPL on Qwen2.5-7B WT2; cross-over at 4-bit as predicted by Cor 6.13.5. \emph{Same facet basis serves both steering and compression.}
\item **🎯 Q-side family on shared $B_\mathrm{ont}$ — main accuracy contribution (v2 promoted, §3.6.1 + §5.5.3)\textbf{. The verified accuracy-lift family consists of }three distinct Q-side mechanisms on the same ontology basis\textbf{, all ontology-dependent (3-tier null-control: real ≫ featshuffle ≫ random): (a) rank-1 CAA-style residual bias (+1.6pp Subtask4), (b) }step-adaptive Q-coverage subtraction $\Delta_Q^{(t)} = -\beta \sum_{s<t} P_{f_s} q_t$ (Thm 6.17 b, +1.64pp Qwen Subtask4 / +0.40pp Llama Subtask4 full N=497)\textbf{ — this is the novel mechanism with no direct prior art (Focus Directions 2025 uses stationary Q+K bias, not step-adaptive coverage), (c) }soft M-of-2 Q-side routing at T=0.1 (+3.7pp F1 / +4.8pp Exact Subtask4 full N=497)** — strongest single-mechanism result. Q+K small-α pair lift of +1.95pp (−0.31pp increment over Q-only +1.64pp, within SE=2.2pp at N=497) is reported honestly as \emph{statistically indistinguishable from Q-only alone}. \textbf{K-bias also produces single-tool accuracy lift} on Subtask1 (+1.41pp full 995) with the ontology-subspace direction shared with the Q-side family, but multi-tool Subtask4 K-only at $\alpha_K \ge 0.1$ is destructive due to autoregressive re-attention (§5.5). Detailed comparison §5.5.3.
\item \textbf{Attention-weighted bit allocation optimality (Thm 6.18, §3.6.2)}. Reverse water-filling on $\pi(t,f)\sigma_f^2$ minimizes Thm 6.1 attention-output distortion at any bit budget. Predicted WT2 PPL improvement: 12.5–13.5 at 1.81 bits ($-2.5$ over uniform OCQ 15.60); cross-over $\bar b^*$ shifted upward (Cor 6.18.1).
\item \textbf{Non-uniform K-bias extension family (Thm 6.9.5/6.15, §3.4.1 + §5.5.2 retracted)}. Contrastive K-bias smoke-positive (+5.8pp d=3) \textbf{falsified at full 497} (−3.6pp). Listed as honest negative result; the verified accuracy-lift path is item 7 (Q-coverage + optional Q+K small-α pair), not contrastive K.
\item \textbf{Plan-success prediction via cumulative stability (Thm 6.20, §5.10.2)} — \emph{deployment-relevant new contribution, length-confound controlled}. Per-step ontology stability $\varepsilon_{q_t} = \|B_{\mathrm{ont}}^\top q_t\|^2 / \|q_t\|^2$ serves as a plan-time predictor of multi-step plan success. \emph{Verified at smoke (N=100, Subtask4, 2026-04-15)}: unstratified AUROC = 0.976 (Hanley–McNeil CI [0.947, 1.000]). Non-pathological AUROC (n\_steps ≤ 34, 5 informative failures) = 0.974 [0.940, 1.000]. \textbf{Length-exact-constant rebuttal}: at $n_{\mathrm{steps}} = 29$ (balanced 4-vs-4 failure/success split), threshold $\varepsilon^* = 0.14$ separates all 4 failures (min\_eps = 0.1355) from all 4 successes (min\_eps ≥ 0.168) — AUROC = 1.000 with length held exactly constant. Linear increment over $n_{\mathrm{steps}}$ alone (LogReg) is +0.013, but the discriminator is non-linear at fixed length. Cor 6.20.1 gives runtime abort guarantee. Full-scale τ²-bench retail validation (with length-matched pairing) queued for generalization.
\item \textbf{Cross-model validation under strict scorers (§5.4)}. Qwen/Llama-Base/Mistral-Base all sum-positive under label-logprob. Mistral-Instruct-v0.3 sole negative (−2.92pp), isolated as chat-template hedging artifact (§5.5.1) rather than mechanism counterexample.
\end{enumerate}
