## EN draft (paper `what_scale_buys` — method/related-work)

**Long-horizon reliability as a product, and the per-step mechanism it requires.**
A long-horizon agent succeeds only if a long conjunction of per-step judgments all hold, so end-to-end success decays as the product of per-step reliabilities and collapses even at high single-step accuracy (H_s(p)=⌈ln s / ln p⌉; Sinha & Geiping, arXiv:2509.09677). A now-standard family of methods attacks this by **decomposition**: cutting the task into minimal, context-isolated subtasks so that a local error cannot propagate (Maximal Agentic Decomposition, MAKER; Task-Decoupled Planning, arXiv:2601.07577; Tree/Graph-of-Thoughts, arXiv:2305.10601). Decomposition, however, only reshapes the chain; it still requires a **per-step correctness mechanism** to keep each conjunct reliable. Existing methods supply this with *statistical* or *self-evaluative* signals — first-to-ahead-by-k **voting** (MAKER), which costs k× samples per step and remains probabilistic, or LLM **self-evaluation** of candidate thoughts (ToT/GoT), which is known to be unreliable: intrinsic self-correction without external feedback does not improve, and can degrade, model outputs (Huang et al., arXiv:2310.01798; Kamoi et al., TACL 2024).

**Our contribution: a per-step router over three correctness levers {voting, verify, ASK}.**
We adopt the decomposition substrate as-is and contribute the per-step correctness mechanism it lacks. The key observation is that the statistical mechanism decomposition relies on — resampling and majority voting — **extracts signal from variance**, and is therefore *vacuous on the errors that dominate our residual*. Both canonical voting results are explicit about this precondition. Self-consistency "leverages the intuition that a complex reasoning problem typically admits multiple different ways of thinking leading to its unique correct answer" (Wang et al., arXiv:2203.11171) — i.e. diverse paths converge on the correct answer as the mode. MAKER's million-step, zero-error result rests on "the statistical power of independent samples from a stochastic process (here an LLM)" and, for its analysis, "assumed that the errors are i.i.d. across steps" (§3.2); crucially the authors place the case we target outside their reach, tying it to model-training side-effects: "Such strange behaviors for particular inputs are well-known side-effects of LLM training, and dealing with such correlated errors is an open foundational problem in machine learning" (§5) (arXiv:2511.09030). Our residual is exactly that case: the errors are *systematic* (a shared prior or absent information), so every resample returns the **same** wrong answer and the wrong answer is the mode. We measured this directly — on the intent-selection / reference-matching residual, self-consistency yields **+0%** with **8-of-8 unanimous identical errors** (whereas serial CoT yields +4%); zero variance leaves resampling nothing to aggregate, and the unanimity simultaneously defeats any disagreement-flag. This matches the theoretical bound that parallel voting cannot add serial-computation depth (Mirtaheri et al., arXiv:2505.21825). The point is not that voting is useless — it is optimal where per-step errors are stochastic and high-variance (MAKER's mechanical Towers-of-Hanoi steps) — but that its applicability is *conditional on the error regime*, and that regime is measurable.

We therefore keep voting as a first-class lever and contribute the **router that assigns each step to the mechanism its error regime admits**: {voting | deterministic verify | ASK}. The routing signal is *voting-recoverability*, measured by resampling a step k times from a fixed context and asking two questions: (a) does the sampled distribution have variance, and (b) is the correct answer recoverable — is the majority vote correct, or is the correct answer at least in the sample support? Three regimes result. **Voting-solvable**: the correct answer is the mode across diverse samples — use voting (the MAKER regime). **Verify**: the step is *decidable* (checkable/computable from context) but the model's error is systematic — the wrong answer is the mode or the correct answer is a minority — so we **change the computation** with a prior-independent deterministic check that a systematic bias cannot survive and that a re-vote over the same biased computation cannot reach. **ASK**: the correct answer is absent from the sample support and the step is *non-decidable* (the information is exogenous), the regime where intrinsic self-correction provably fails (Huang et al., arXiv:2310.01798) — so we supply the missing information by asking the user. Two distinct correlations must not be conflated here: the routing signal is *within-step, across-resample* correlation (the voting-vacuity sense), which is orthogonal to *across-step self-conditioning* (the horizon/trajectory sense; cf. the category error flagged by "Too Consistent to Detect," arXiv:2505.17656). At training/analysis time the regimes are labeled against gold; the runtime router is gold-free, using decidability (a structural property declared by the tool schema) plus resample entropy as a confidence proxy.

**Bounding the ASK budget with an information-theoretic floor.**
Routing every non-decidable conjunct to a question risks a second failure mode — dialogue inflation leading the user to abandon the task. We bound this with the irreducible information the user must supply, an information-theoretic floor H_min (per dispute type, banking: 4.27 bits for debit, 2.60 for credit; a full interaction ≈ 11–18 bits ≈ 5–7 questions), and fold the *effective* number of questions down toward that floor: fields determined by others are **derived** rather than re-asked (≈18 zero-information fields), fields with a dominant value take a **default** (e.g. police-report ≈ always false), and the remainder are ordered by value of information. Because H_min is a structural property of the task, this residual is a **non-scaling** lever: a larger base model does not shrink the bits the user alone can provide, which is why a small model equipped with this deterministic mechanism can close the gap to a frontier model on the compliance/horizon residual.

**Scope and what remains to measure.**
The central empirical object is the **per-step regime partition** itself: what fraction of failing steps are voting-solvable (already covered by MAKER-style aggregation) versus verify-needed versus ASK-needed. This is measured with an isolated resample probe — fix the context at a decision point, sample the step k times, and record variance and gold-recoverability (majority-vote correctness; gold-in-support) — at zero paid-simulation cost. One bucket is already labeled: on the reference-anchoring residual, resampling yields +0% with 8-of-8 unanimous errors and no gold recovery, i.e. **not voting-solvable → verify (via reference-filter) or ASK** (probe6, graded [M]). We report the composition as four claims with distinct evidence status: (i) voting is vacuous on the systematic (zero-variance) residual — **settled [M]**, and remains the correct lever on the stochastic regime; (ii) the three-way partition over banking buckets (⋈ / compute / coverage / gather) is measurable and quantifies the whitespace — **⋈ done, rest to measure (free/local)**; (iii) on the decidable-but-systematic subset, deterministic verify closes steps voting cannot, but only where *correctly formalizable* — mis-formalization injects a **new** systematic error (false confidence), so Δspurious ≤ 0 must be measured, not assumed; (iv) the ASK budget folds to a measured information-theoretic floor via DERIVE/DEFAULT/VOI — **H_min settled [S], end-to-end fold to measure**. The runtime router (decidability + resample entropy, gold-free) is the patentable mechanism; voting is included in its lever set rather than displaced.

## ★2층 라우터 (2026-07-15·C90·⋈=plan 실측 후 정련)
E-REGIME ⋈ 실측(plan 67.9%·verify 1.6%)이 라우터가 **한 층이 아님**을 드러냄. 다중-item 과제는 2층:
- **Outer loop (across-item·E-PLAN)** = 항목(dispute) 열거→**격리**(per-item 컨텍스트 리셋)→완료추적. 측정한 plan-앵커링(560·다중-dispute)이 격리로 소멸. **loop=결정론 컨트롤러**(프롬프트 아님·LLM은 일찍 종료=under-action·C80·[[07]]).
- **Inner router (within-item·per-step)** = §2의 {voting|verify|ASK}. 각 격리된 item 안에서 correctness(compute→verify·pin→ASK·stochastic→voting).
- **상보(비중첩)**: outer=item 간 plan·inner=item 내 correctness.
- **loop의 공짜 아닌 2지점**: ①**열거/discovery**(무엇을 돌지=reach 잔여·C80 미스 80%=발견실패) ②**결정론 강제**(loop을 LLM 자율에 안 맡김). 격리 자체는 선행 decomposition(양보). ①②=딥리서치 `wf_23666af5-a45` 선행조사 중.
