\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Serial-position effects in LLMs.}
\citet{guo2024serial} name serial-position effects for option-selection tasks (label choice,
primacy-dominated, mitigation inconsistent). \citet{wang2023primacy} report label-choice primacy in ChatGPT;
listwise-reranking position bias is documented in IR. Our task is per-item enumerated judgment, not option
selection, and we show the driver is not the position itself.

\paragraph{Batch prompting.}
\citet{cheng2023batch} report accuracy degradation as batch size grows ($\approx$4-item threshold) and per-item
answers that depend on batch position; BatchPrompt \citep{lin2023batchprompt} mitigates by permutation and
voting. These works frame the effect as lost-in-the-middle over absolute positions \citep{liu2023lost} and do
not separate token position from list ordinality, nor manipulate inter-item similarity---the two dissociations
that localize the cause here.

\paragraph{Instruction-following at scale.}
IFScale \citep{jaroslawicz2025ifscale} shows primacy favoritism over 500 simultaneous instructions. Consistent
with our ``edges are protected'' observation, but no interference design.

\paragraph{Proactive interference.}
Unable to Forget \citep{wang2025unable} establishes log-linear PI decay in key--value overwrite recall;
Remember First Forget Last \citep{rffl2026} shows primacy protection and recency collapse. Both use explicit
overwrite memory probes. We show PI-like interference \textbf{without any overwriting}, in a document-grounded
judgment task, gated by \emph{clause-level semantic similarity}---and we import the classic release-from-PI
manipulation \citep{watkins1975cue}, and the fan-effect lineage \citep{anderson1974fan}, from cognitive
psychology to prove it.

\paragraph{Practitioner folklore.}
``Split calls beyond $\sim$7 items'' circulates as guidance. We upgrade folklore to a measured threshold, an
identified unit of interference, and a mitigation with quantified non-regression.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
We claim neither the discovery of position effects nor of PI in LLMs. The claims are the unit (rule clause),
the dissociation methodology, the failure mode (default retreat with threshold instability), the end-to-end
task connection, and the mechanism-derived fix.
